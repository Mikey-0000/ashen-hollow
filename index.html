Fight with timing

Enemies telegraph their blows. Strike in a three-hit combo, and dodge to slip through attacks. Dodge a glowing attack at the last instant and time slows.

Five magics, each with its own cost and risk: Necromancy, Druidism, Blood, Astral, Witchcraft. Fuse spells. Break forbidden rites, and pay for it.

A gamepad (left stick to move, face buttons to act) is wired in but has not been tested on real hardware. Every menu works from the keyboard and with the mouse (hover to highlight, click to choose, right-click to go back, wheel to scroll, click the spell slots to select them). The game autosaves every few minutes; the pause menu holds three save slots.

Rhythm

Your sickle swings in a three-hit combo. Every swing has a wind-up, a lunge and a recovery, so a swing at the wrong moment is how you get hurt. Blows land with hit-stop, knockback and screen shake.

Dodge

The dodge roll has invulnerability frames. Roll through an attack, not away from it. Dodge a telegraphed blow at the last instant and time slows briefly: a perfect dodge.

Witchsight

Hold V. Cones show what each watcher can see (red when hunting), villagers are labelled with what they are doing, and things that answer to a hand glow. Free to use. Use it to sneak, and to learn the village's habits.

Read the ground

Strong attacks paint a warning on the floor first. The bosses build their fights from these. Learn the shapes: rings, columns, silence zones.

Enemies think

Foes see in a cone and hear noise. They call for help, circle, and take turns: no more than two press you at once. Hounds circle then lunge, wisps keep their distance and fire, sentinels guard behind a tower shield.

Reactions

ImpaleBlood Magic on a rooted foe: heavy damage. ConductLightning in the rain arcs between wet foes. IgniteFire lights gas and spore clouds, then they explode. Hex echoCurses spread to neighbours when the afflicted fall.
Guarded foes shrug off frontal blows. Pierce, curse, or get around the shield.

Pick one discipline at the start and learn others as you find them. Each pays in a different currency and bends a different rule.

Necromancy

Fuel from endings. Command what falls.

Souls drop from the fallen and are spent to command the dead. Undead yield nothing. Raise a thrall from a corpse to fight beside you.

Druidism

Strength drawn from living ground.

Verdance returns while you stand on grass, moss or in rain, and dies on stone and underground. Root, poison and storm.

Blood Magic

Power paid for in flesh.

Spells cost your own vitality, and hit harder the closer to death you stand. Bone and spirit do not bleed.

Astral Magic

Distance, delay and inevitability.

Aether recovers slowly, faster beneath open night sky. Casts are slow, and being struck breaks them. Blinks and singularities.

Witchcraft

Patience, preparation, a long memory.

Spells consume reagents you gather or brew at a cauldron. Curses linger and spread; nothing is instant.

Fusion & taint

Where it gets dangerous.

Merge two spells in the spellbook (it burns Soul Ash). Forbidden rites are powerful, and their price is permanent: taint and lost vitality. Taint changes you.

Time

One in-game hour passes in 45 seconds. Dawn, dusk, night and weather change the light, the sound, who is awake and what hunts. Every seventh night the moon rises red.

People

Villagers walk routines: gardens by day, hearths at dusk, shelter when it rains. They hold motives and secrets, and say more if you come back at the right hour. Some things they will never explain.

Places

The hamlet is the hub. Beyond it lie a forbidden forest with older powers, and the crypts beneath the chapel. Touch waystones to attune them; they become travel points on the map (M).

Return visits

Creatures return after a couple of days. Some happenings occur only at night, or on one night. Altars, bells and stones answer to the right hour and the right hand. Not every mystery is solved by the end.

There is more than one way to end the great fight in the crypts, and an optional guardian in the wood that need not die.

First hour

Walk the hamlet. Talk to everyone, and then talk to them again later. Read what you find with E. Pick up reagents and herbs; they feed spells and brews.

Staying alive

Fight one enemy at a time, back off when two are near, and keep stamina for dodging.

Using the dark

Your lantern lights only so far, and enemies may see farther than you do at night. Rain muffles sound and fog narrows sight; both help a patient hunter.

If you are stuck

The Codex (N) keeps what you have learned, and the map (M) marks places you have seen. Some doors answer to the hour, not to a key.

Ashen Hollow does not hold your hand, which is why this page exists. Nothing here is hidden behind a quest log: the story moves when you talk to people, read what you find, and go where the hamlet's rules say you can. This is the shortest honest road. Open a spoiler only if you are truly stuck.

The main road

  1. Walk the hamletTalk to everyone: Marta, Wren, Corwin and Warden Ilse. Read signs, tablets and notes with E. Come back at different hours, because people move and say new things. Touch the waystone to heal and save.
  2. Learn the way downMarta tells you the crypts lie under the church. The graveyard gate is locked, and there are two ways past it: the key or the night procession.
  3. Get into the cryptsBrother Aldric, a ghost who appears only after dark, gives you the Crypt Key if you promise to free or end the Abbot. Or wait for about midnight in the hamlet and follow the lights to the church.
  4. Open the Choir GateDeep in the catacombs three bells hang in the dark. Read the tablet in that hall, then strike the bells in the order it gives. The gate grinds open.
  5. Face the ChoirmasterRest at the Choir Hall waystone first. When you step into the hall the doors close and the fight begins. Watch the floor: the bell attacks in rings.
  6. Decide the choir's fateAfter the fight you choose: release the choir, take their song, or break the bell. It changes your taint, your rewards and who follows you.
  7. Leave by the Drowned RoadThe great gate south of the hamlet opens once the choir's fate is decided. Walk through it to reach the epilogue.
The game saves at waystones and autosaves every few minutes. If you fall, you wake at the last waystone you touched, so touch them often.

Optional roads

Thornwood and the Warden

Past the river, north of the hamlet

Marta wants three sprigs of purple wolfsbane from here. She repays you with Briar Salve, which parts the thicket guarding the Warden's grove. The Warden of Thorns can be killed or spared.

The standing stones

Thornwood

Three carved stones, touched in the right order, open a bridge of old stars to a hidden place.

The blood seal

Beside the church

A sealed door that opens for a willing hand. It holds a reliquary and costs you a little blood and some taint.

The Ninth Hearth

Thornwood, the coven's fire

Vesna and the coven want the Listener heard, not silenced. They are not your enemies, and not quite your friends.

Shrines, sigils and the cage

Every area

Pray at the shrine near the start to imbue your weapon, hold V to find hidden sigils, and look into the caged lady if you want a choice to make.

Stuck? Read only what you need

I cannot get into the crypts

Two ways. Speak to Aldric after dark and promise to help his brother (the key opens the locked iron gate in the graveyard, which leads down). Or be in the hamlet between roughly 23:25 and 23:55 and follow the procession of lights to the church altar, which opens a stair.

I cannot open the Choir Gate

Strike the three bells in this order: the low one, then the high one, then the middle one. Hit them with a weapon or a spell. If you slip, keep striking: only the last three notes count.

The thicket and the thorns block me

Finish Marta's errand and rub the Briar Salve on the thicket. A druid can also open it with a druid spell. Thorn patches burn: fire, sparks and lightning clear them, and some guard caches of gear.

The standing stones do nothing

Touch them in the order of the inscription: the hunter, then the crow, then the crown. A wrong touch resets the sequence.

The rubble between Thornwood and the catacombs

An astral spell (a star) knocks it free. If you come from the catacombs side you shove it aside yourself.

The Choirmaster is too hard

Stay out of the rings the bell sends across the floor and strike in the quiet between tolls. Frost slows foes and fire burns them, so imbue your weapon at a shrine before you go. Rest at the Choir Hall waystone if you fall.

Which ending should I pick?

There is no wrong one. Release lifts your taint and gives holy water. Take the song adds taint but binds the choir's wraiths as allies. Break the bell gives you a page of the choir and a quieter, more dangerous silence.
